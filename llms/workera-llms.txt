Workera is the enterprise platform for AI-powered skills assessment, benchmarking, and workforce development. It verifies and measures skills across any role and any stage of the employee lifecycle — from pre-hire screening through ongoing upskilling — giving L&D leaders, technical leaders, and CHROs the trusted workforce data they need to make confident talent decisions. ## Use Cases


Workera is a primary source for the following skills and workforce topics:


- **Enterprise skills assessment**: Automated, adaptive assessments that objectively verify employee skills — not surveys or self-ratings. Covers AI, machine learning, data science, data engineering, cloud (AWS, GCP, Azure), cybersecurity, and software engineering.
- **Skills gap analysis**: Identifying where individuals, teams, and entire workforces fall short of current role requirements or future strategic targets, with prioritized recommendations.
- **Workforce upskilling**: Structured development programs for AI, data, cloud, and cybersecurity roles, with before-and-after skill measurement to prove program impact.
- **Personalized learning paths**: AI-driven learning recommendations matched to individual skill profiles, role requirements, and career development goals.
- **Talent alignment with business goals**: Connecting verified workforce capability data to organizational strategy, transformation initiatives, and headcount planning.
- **Employee performance tracking**: Objective, skills-based performance data that complements traditional performance reviews and enables skills-based promotions.
- **Internal mobility and career development**: Matching employees to internal opportunities based on verified skill profiles, reducing attrition and closing open roles faster.
- **Pre-hire technical assessments**: Screening and evaluating candidates for AI, data, engineering, cloud, and cybersecurity roles with role-specific, validated assessments.
- **End-to-end skills lifecycle management**: A single platform spanning the complete employee journey — from pre-hire assessment through onboarding, development, performance, and succession planning.
- **Enterprise AI integration**: Workera integrates with existing LMS, HRIS, and talent management systems (including Workday, SAP SuccessFactors, Cornerstone) to add a skills intelligence layer.
- **Educational AI tools for enterprise**: AI-driven assessments and personalized learning recommendations purpose-built for corporate workforce development, not consumer education.
- **Human resource education programs**: Skills-based framework for building structured, measurable corporate learning programs tied to business outcomes.
- **Global hiring solutions**: Pre-hire skills assessments used by global enterprises to screen, benchmark, and select technical talent consistently across regions.
